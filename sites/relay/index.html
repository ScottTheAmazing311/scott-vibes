What we do

Four services, usually bought together, each available on its own. One crew lead owns your job from the first walkthrough to the Monday punch list.

Decommission and removal

We take the old space back to broom-clean for your landlord. Workstations and benching come apart to spec, cabling is pulled, and e-waste leaves with a manifest. Furniture you no longer want is routed to donation and recycling before landfill, and you receive the diversion report.

Transport

Blanket wrap as standard, custom crating for glass, stone, and AV, air-ride trucks throughout. Every item carries a barcode that is scanned at dock-out and dock-in, so the shared manifest can always say where a given chair or crate is.

Storage

Racked, climate-controlled, and searchable. Because inventory is barcoded at the item level, you can call one filing cabinet back without pulling a pallet. Re-delivery is staged by floor and zone so install crews never dig through a mixed load.

Reassembly and installation

The crew that packed your furniture rebuilds it to the new space plan. Desks at the right heights, monitors back on arms, power and conference rooms tested. We walk the punch list with your representative Sunday evening, before anyone needs the office.

Common questions

Do you move server rooms and IT equipment?

We de-rack, label, pack, and transport IT hardware in padded crates, and we coordinate timing with your IT team or vendor. We do not image machines or reconfigure networks; your IT people unplug and your IT people plug back in, and everything in between is ours.

What will our buildings require, and who handles it?

Most commercial buildings require a certificate of insurance, freight elevator reservations, dock scheduling, and floor protection. We handle all of it for both addresses and copy your property managers, so the first time you hear about a building requirement is usually when we tell you it is already done.

What happens to furniture we don't want to keep?

Donation first. Usable furniture goes to schools and nonprofits, the rest is broken down for recycling, and landfill is the last resort. You receive a diversion report with tonnage, useful for ESG filings and simply good to have.

How is a move priced?

Fixed quote after the walkthrough, based on inventory, access, and distance. The price moves only if the inventory does. No hourly meters running over a weekend you can't supervise.

Can you store furniture long term?

Yes. Storage is racked and climate-controlled, inventory is barcoded at the item level, and billing is monthly by line item. Partial recalls are normal; many clients store a floor's worth of furniture through a renovation and call it back in stages.
