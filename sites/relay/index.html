Scope

Four jobs. One manifest.

LOT 01

Decommission & removal

We take the old office down to broom-clean. Panel systems and benching broken to spec, cabling pulled, e-waste manifested. Furniture you keep gets tagged for the truck; furniture you shed gets routed to donation and recycling first, and you get the tonnage report for your sustainability filing.

  • Panel & benching teardown
  • Donate-first disposition
  • Landlord broom-clean handback
LOT 02

Transport

Air-ride trucks, blanket wrap and custom crating for stone, glass, and AV. Every asset carries a barcode and is scanned at dock-out and dock-in, so the manifest answers "where is it" at any hour of the weekend.

  • Air-ride fleet
  • Chain-of-custody scans
  • Crating for glass, stone, AV
LOT 03

Secure storage

When the new space isn't ready, your inventory waits in racked, climate-controlled storage, barcoded by asset and retrievable by line item. Re-delivery is staged by floor so install crews never dig through a mixed load.

  • Racked & climate-controlled
  • Retrieval by line item
  • Staged re-delivery by floor
LOT 04

Reassembly & install

Desks, benching, monitor arms, and seating rebuilt to the space plan, chairs at the right desks, conference rooms live. We walk the punch list with you Sunday night, not Monday noon.

  • Rebuild to space plan
  • Monitor arms & power re-set
  • Sunday punch-list walk
